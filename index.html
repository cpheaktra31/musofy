ME

Nobara Kugisaki

The Fearless Sorcerer

Nobara Kugisaki is a powerful and confident jujutsu sorcerer from Jujutsu Kaisen, created by Gege Akutami. Born on August 7, Nobara grew up in a small countryside village before moving to Tokyo to pursue her dream of living independently.

As a first-year student at Tokyo Jujutsu High, she fights alongside Yuji Itadori and Megumi Fushiguro under the guidance of Satoru Gojo.

Armed with her signature hammer, nails, and Straw Doll Technique, Nobara battles curses with fierce determination and fearless confidence.

Proud of her identity and unwilling to compromise her values, Nobara believes in living life on her own terms.

“I love myself when I'm pretty and all dressed up! And I love myself when I'm being strong!”
